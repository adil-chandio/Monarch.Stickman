# Video source and attribution

`Prehistoric_Fire_Reference_8s_720p.mp4` is a silent, 8-second, 1280×720 excerpt of the existing prehistoric-fire animation. It is **not a newly drawn animation**. The GIF supplied by the user corresponds to approximately 00:01–00:09 of the original 30-second video; this edit uses that segment at the original video's higher resolution, preserves its English/Chinese on-screen captions, and removes the audio as requested.
